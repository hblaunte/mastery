# Why the interface is obstructive in the places it is

Mastery is built on Bjork, Dunlosky & Kornell (2013), *Self-Regulated Learning: Beliefs,
Techniques, and Illusions*, Annual Review of Psychology 64, 417–444. Each design decision below
names the finding it comes from, so that if something feels like friction you can decide whether
to keep it with the reason in view.

1. **The library is a peer of "Begin session", never a step before it.** Rereading produces
   fluency without learning; the familiarity of the text is mistaken for knowing it. The reading
   list appears only after a miss, pointed at the passage the item came from.
2. **Predict before you answer.** The judgement of learning taken before an attempt, compared with
   the score, is the calibration measurement. Taking it after would contaminate it with the attempt.
3. **The answer box does not appear until you have predicted.** Otherwise the prediction is skipped.
4. **Reflect after you answer, before the grade.** The drop from prediction to reflection is
   foresight bias made visible: the answer felt available at study time and would not come.
5. **Nothing is graded until the whole session is in.** Per-item feedback turns the rest of the
   session into recognition rather than retrieval, and is the most common design mistake in study
   software.
6. **Detents on the confidence picker, with verbal anchors.** A slider invites a shrug at the
   midpoint; a flat 75 across a session has no variance and therefore no resolution to compute.
7. **Expanding intervals, collapsed by a lapse.** Spaced retrieval beats massed practice; the
   interval should be long enough to be effortful and short enough to usually succeed.
8. **Interleaving across courses.** Blocked practice feels more fluent and teaches less.
9. **Grading that refuses to reward fluent prose.** The illusion of competence from fluency is
   the central illusion the paper describes; a grader that credits it corrupts the calibration
   record in the direction you are least able to notice from the inside.
10. **The graph lights on production, not recognition.** See graph-threshold.md.
