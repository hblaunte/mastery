First launch

Mac: open the .dmg and drag Mastery to Applications. If macOS says the app cannot be checked, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again. That is only needed once.

Windows: run the installer. If SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

What you need

  1. An Anthropic API key. Mastery calls Claude with your key, from your machine. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use; a course costs well under a dollar to build, a study session a few cents.
  2. Something to learn from. PDF, Word, PowerPoint, EPUB, Markdown or a web page. Scanned PDFs are transcribed on request.
  3. Ten minutes a day. The app tells you what is due. Everything you study stays on your computer.
